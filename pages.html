Welcome to Smart Cattle Health AI

Smart Cattle Health AI is an AI-powered computer vision system designed to detect possible cattle diseases from images.

The system supports both Cow and Buffalo using separately trained YOLO object detection models.

The detected disease region is displayed using bounding boxes together with the confidence score and risk level.

AI Disease Detection

Select the animal, upload an image and allow the trained AI model to analyze the image.

The system identifies possible disease regions using object detection.

🐄 Cow Health

Cows are important livestock animals, especially for dairy production and agriculture.

Regular health monitoring can help identify possible health problems at an early stage.

🐃 Buffalo Health

Buffalo are important dairy animals and contribute significantly to milk production.

Monitoring the skin, udder, mouth and legs can help identify possible health problems.

Detected Disease Classes

1. Foot-and-Mouth Disease

2. Healthy

3. Lumpy Skin Disease

4. Mastitis

About the Project

This project uses computer vision and deep learning to assist with cattle health screening.

Cow and Buffalo use separate trained YOLO models because their datasets and training processes are maintained independently.

The system is designed as an AI-assisted screening tool and does not replace professional veterinary diagnosis.
