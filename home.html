ABOUT THE PROJECT

Smart Cattle Health AI

Smart Cattle Health AI is an AI-powered system that detects common cattle diseases in cows and buffaloes using computer vision. It provides early warnings, risk information and practical health guidance to support better cattle care and timely veterinary attention.

AI POWERED HEALTHCARE

Key Capabilities

🐄
Cow & Buffalo Detection
AI models support health monitoring for both cows and buffaloes.
🔬
Disease Detection
Detects Foot-and-Mouth Disease, Lumpy Skin Disease, Mastitis and healthy cases.
⚕️
Early Health Guidance
Provides preliminary risk information and practical health guidance for cattle care.
